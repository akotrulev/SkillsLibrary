# Requirements — Session credits

**Requested by:** Maria Costa, studio owner  **Date:** 2026-09-28  **Status:** Approved

## 1. The ask, verbatim
> Members should get a number of session credits every month and spend them on bookings. When they run out they can still book, and we charge the difference. Cancelling in good time should give the credits back. We need to set the plans ourselves.

## 2. Problem & why now
Today every booking is paid for one by one, and members who attend often pay the same as those who rarely come. The studio wants a monthly allowance so that regulars pay less per session and the month's extra bookings are charged once, at the month's end.

## 3. Actors
| Actor | What they are trying to get done | Role in this initiative |
|-------|----------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Member | Book and cancel sessions without paying per booking | primary user |
| Studio staff | Set up plans and rates, cancel a session when it cannot run | primary user |
| Studio owner | Approve the rules and the month-end charge | decision-maker |

## 4. What it must do
- A plan gives a member a monthly allowance of session credits: Basic 8, Plus 16.
- A booking spends credits by the session's tier: Standard 1, Workshop 2, Masterclass 3 per seat.
- A booking is never refused for lack of credits. The part the credits do not cover is an overage, charged per credit: £6.00 on Basic, £5.00 on Plus. A member's personal overage rate, when one is set, is used instead of the plan's.
- A member can see their plan, credits used, credits left and the overage waiting on their account.
- A member who cancels a booking 24 hours or more before the session starts gets the credits back and the overage for that booking is removed. Later than that, nothing is returned. The cut-off gives the studio a day to offer the seat to another member.
- When staff cancel a session, every booked member gets their credits back, however close the session is.
- Staff can change a plan's name, monthly credits and overage rate. A plan name is unique. Credits and rate cannot be below 0.
- Staff can set and clear a member's personal overage rate.
- Each member with overage sees one charge for the month's total, posted on the last day of the month.
- Credits not used by the end of the month carry over to the next month, up to 4.
- A booking is priced once, with the rates in force when it is confirmed; a later change to a plan does not re-price it.

## 5. Constraints
- Amounts are in pounds (GBP) — stated by the studio owner.

## 6. Success
- Members book without being turned away, and staff no longer chase per-booking payments — judged by the studio owner after the first full month.

## 7. Scope
| In scope | Out of scope (refused) | Deferred (follow-up) |
|----------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Plans, tiers, booking cost, cancelling, session cancelling, month-end charge | Taking the payment itself | Changing the cost of a tier |

## 8. Assumptions
1. Every member is on one plan.
