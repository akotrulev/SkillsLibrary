# E1 — Members spend credits on sessions

**Status:** Approved
**Covers:** requirements.md §4 — the allowance, the booking cost, the overage, seeing credits, cancelling

## 1. Goal (one sentence)
When this epic is done, **members** can **book and cancel sessions against a monthly allowance of credits** so that **a busy month never turns them away**.

## 2. In plain English
**Who it is for and why**
Members who come often want to pay once for a month and book freely, and to give a seat back when their plans change.

**What gets built**
A monthly allowance of credits, a cost for each kind of session, a charge for what the allowance does not cover, and a way to cancel and get credits back in good time.

**How it fits with everything else**
It builds on the booking of seats members already have, and the month-end charge in the next epic collects what it accrues.

## 6. What is included (the stories)
| # | Story | What it holds | Adds |
|---|-------|---------------|------|
| 1 | Booking a session with credits | the cost by tier, the overage, seeing credits | First: the capability to book against credits |
| 2 | Cancelling a booking | credits back in good time, a cancelled session | The way out |

# E2 — Staff run the plans and the month end

**Status:** Approved
**Covers:** requirements.md §4 — plans and rates, the month-end charge, carrying credits over

## 1. Goal (one sentence)
When this epic is done, **staff** can **set up plans and rates and rely on one charge at the month's end** so that **members are charged once and the studio chases nothing**.

## 2. In plain English
**Who it is for and why**
Studio staff who set the prices and the studio owner who wants the month's extra bookings collected in one go.

**What gets built**
Plan settings for staff, and a month-end charge with credits starting afresh.

**How it fits with everything else**
It sets the numbers E1 uses and collects what E1 accrues.

## 6. What is included (the stories)
| # | Story | What it holds | Adds |
|---|-------|---------------|------|
| 3 | Plans and rates | plan settings, personal rates | The numbers E1 uses |
| 4 | The month end | one charge, credits starting again | Collecting the month |
